About this Insta360 I-Log LUT tool

This free tool creates and tunes 3D LUTs for footage shot in Insta360 I-Log. You can start from Insta360's official LUT and add exposure, white balance, contrast, saturation, lift/gamma/gain and highlight rolloff, or build a LUT directly from the I-Log curve. Output can be Rec.709, Rec.2020 SDR, HLG or PQ, exported as .cube or .3dl. It was inspired by GoPro Labs' GP-Log2 LUT generator.

How do I use it?

Optionally load the official I-Log LUT, choose a mode, adjust the sliders while watching the preview and scopes, then press Save as .zip and load the .cube in your editor. Set your footage to I-Log first.

Does it work with the Insta360 X5?

The curve and color gamut in this tool come from Insta360's published 10-bit I-Log white paper, which covers the Luna series and other newer cameras. The author has checked it on Insta360 X5 ProRes I-Log footage and the curve appears to work. Insta360's paper does not list every camera, so if you have the official LUT for yours, load it and press Match official LUT to see how closely they agree, and check the result on your own footage.

Where do I get the official Insta360 I-Log LUTs?

From Insta360's I-Log downloads page.

Is my footage uploaded?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Is this official?

No. This is an independent, unofficial tool and is not affiliated with Insta360 or GoPro. It was written with the help of AI and has not been tested on all footage, so check results before relying on them. Source code and notes are on GitHub.
